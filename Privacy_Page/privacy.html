Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the AeroSim website uses and how it is handled. AeroSim is a college project (CSE 326), so this policy is written in simple words.

In short: AeroSim has no server and no database. Everything you type is saved only inside your own browser, on your own device. We never receive it.

1. Information we collect

You may enter the following information while using the website:

  • Register page: your full name, email address and password.
  • Login page: your email address and password.
  • Track Bags: the bag tag number you search for (for example AS1092).
  • Contact page: your name, email address, query type, an optional bag tag number and your message.

2. Where your information is stored

The website saves this information using your browser's localStorage. This is a small storage area inside your own web browser. The information:

  • stays on your device and is not sent to any server;
  • is not shared with, sold to or seen by anyone else;
  • can only be read by this website in the same browser on the same device.

Because of this, accounts created on one browser or device will not appear on another.

3. How the information is used

  • To create your account and log you in.
  • To show your details on the Profile page.
  • To show the sample journey of the bag you search for.
  • To keep the queries you send from the Contact page.

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

4. Cookies and tracking

AeroSim does not use cookies, analytics tools or advertising trackers.

5. Sample data

The baggage details, flights and passenger names shown on the Track Bags page are sample data made for demonstration. They do not belong to real people or real flights.

6. Your password

Because this is a demonstration website, passwords are saved in your browser without strong protection. Please do not use a real password that you use on other websites or for banking, email or any important account.

7. Your choices and control

  • You can log out at any time from the website.
  • You can delete all information saved by AeroSim by clearing the site data (localStorage) in your browser settings.
  • You can choose not to enter any information; you can still read the pages that do not need an account.

8. Children

AeroSim is a student project and is not meant for collecting information from children. Please do not enter personal details of a child.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the project changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

10. Contact us

If you have a question about this policy, please use the Contact page. You may also read our Terms of Service.
