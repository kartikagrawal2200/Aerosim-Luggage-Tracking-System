Track Your Baggage Smartly

Follow each step of your bag’s journey with a quick tag lookup. Our sample tracker gives you a clear view from check-in through arrival.

How It Works

Follow your luggage through the 6 standard airport handling stages from check-in to arrival carousel.

  1. 01

    Check-In Complete

    Luggage tagged with standard IATA barcode.

  2. 02

    Baggage Handling

    Automated X-ray screening and conveyor sorting.

  3. 03

    Aircraft Loading

    Positive baggage match and cargo hold stowage.

  4. 04

    Flight In Transit

    En-route in climate-controlled aircraft hold.

  5. 05

    Aircraft Unloading

    Ground crew marshals containers to arrivals.

  6. 06

    Arrival Claim Stage

    Delivered to designated baggage carousel.
