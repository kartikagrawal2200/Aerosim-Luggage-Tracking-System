About AeroSim

AeroSim is a baggage tracking website. It lets a passenger see where their luggage is during a flight, from check-in at the departure airport until it reaches the arrival belt.

How it works

  1. 1

    Register

    Create an account with your name, email and a password. Your details are saved and shown on your Profile page.

  2. 2

    Login

    Login with your email and password. After a successful login you are taken to the tracking page.

  3. 3

    Enter your bag tag

    Type the bag tag number (for example AS1092) on the Home page to find your bag.

  4. 4

    Follow the journey

    See the flight details and which stage of the journey your bag has reached.

The 6 stages of a bag's journey

  1. Check-in complete: the bag is tagged with a unique barcode.
  2. Baggage handling: the bag goes through security X-ray and sorting.
  3. Aircraft loading: the bag is loaded into the cargo hold.
  4. Flight in transit: the bag is travelling to the destination airport.
  5. Arrival at destination: the bag is unloaded and moved to the terminal.
  6. Arrival claim stage: the bag is on the carousel, ready for collection.
